## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, topography, and quiet hospitality of the Ethiopian highlands, distilled through a disciplined, high-utility digital lens. It merges modern travel-technology precision with an inviting, grounded vernacular. The visual posture avoids tourist cliches, leaning instead into lush highland flora, sunlit stone architecture, and clear structural hierarchy.

The design movement is **Modern Organic Editorial**:
- **Generous spatial breathing room** paired with structural micro-borders that evoke architectural craft and cartographic discipline.
- **Warm tactile surfaces** that prioritize comfort during stressful navigation scenarios, transit delays, or terrain transitions.
- **Multilingual parity** where Latin and Ge'ez (Ethiopic) scripts coexist as equals, designed with optical equilibrium in line height, weight distribution, and rhythmic baseline spacing.
- **Intentional calm** punctuated by functional vitality: amber signals curiosity and assistance, deep evergreen asserts foundational trust, and semantic alerts retain clinical urgency.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of geographic grounding, clarity, and safety across day and night conditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Highland Green (`#1B4332`)**: The anchor of the system, conveying deep stability, regional ecology, and institutional trust. Used for primary interactive actions, high-level headers, and active state highlights.
- **Secondary Harvest Amber (`#D97706`)**: An assertive, radiant accent derived from Meskel celebrations and highland afternoon sunlight. Directs traveler attention to tips, live recommendations, advisory highlights, and quick-action triggers.
- **Tertiary Rift Teal (`#0E7490`)**: Applied selectively to transport vectors, water features, route segments, and informational telemetry badges.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#1C1E1B`)**: High-contrast, warm-tinted ink prioritizing reading comfort across both Latin and Ethiopic typographic strokes.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFAF7`)**: A soft, parchment-tinted warm white that mitigates glare during outdoor mobile usage under intense sunlight.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure cards, modal sheets, and detached floating controls.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#F3F4F0`)**: Tonal grouping containers, search bars, filter strips, and background chips.
- **Border Subtle (`#E2E6DE`)**: A warm-gray hairline separation preventing color vibration between layered surfaces.

### Semantic Allocation
- **Incident & Danger (`#DC2626`)**: Strictly preserved for live road hazard warnings, roadblock advisories, and critical emergency prompts.
- **Confirmation & Safe Passage (`#16A34A`)**: Used for verified checkpoints, completed paths, and safe transit confirmations.
- **Warning & Advisory (`#B45309`)**: Precautionary travel notes, seasonal weather warnings, and unresolved road condition tickets.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered to support trilingual parity across English, አማርኛ (Amharic), and Afaan Oromo without breaking UI density or vertical alignment.

### Font Pairing and Vertical Metrics
- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers geometric warmth with friendly terminals. For Ge'ez script, the system falls back seamlessly to `Noto Sans Ethiopic` or `Abyssinica SIL`. 
- **Body & Functional Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans & Inter)**: Inter is introduced for labels, coordinates, numerical feeds, and status pills to preserve optical legibility at sub-14px sizes.
- **Ge'ez Metric Normalization**: Amharic characters carry taller ascending strokes and broader grapheme profiles. Whenever the active locale is set to Amharic (`am-ET`), line-height values automatically expand by **15% to 20%** across body and label tokens to prevent descender collision and maintain optical rhythm.

### Hierarchical Roles
- **Display & Large Headlines**: Reserved for city anchors (e.g., *Lalibela*, *Gondar*, *Addis Ababa*), regional transition headers, and key weather conditions.
- **Title Tiers**: Guide route summary card groupings, AI query topics, and incident verification banners.
- **Body Tiers**: Optimized for descriptive travel advisories, cultural etiquette briefings, and interactive conversation bubbles.
- **Label Scale**: Houses transit tags, altitude indicators (e.g., `2,355 m a.s.l.`), timeline stamps, and multilingual language switchers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a responsive fluid-grid architecture aligned to an 8px base rhythm (with 4px micro-steps for compact data displays).

### Grid Framework & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `margin-sm` (16px) margins and `gutter-sm` (16px) column channels. Map views occupy full viewport width with floating drawer overlays anchored to the bottom edge.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins and 20px gutters. Dual-pane interactions (map view on top or side-by-side with step-by-step route cards).
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1320px`, enclosed by `margin-lg` outer space. Navigation resides in a fixed left-rail column or a sticky glass-matted header band.

### Reflow Rules & Travel Contexts
- **Map & Feed Split**: On screens wider than 1024px, the interface divides into a 5-column left information feed (itinerary, AI chat, transit warnings) and a 7-column right sticky map/route view.
- **Safe Area Insets**: Floating action pills and mobile route panels strictly calculate dynamic viewport units plus safe-area-inset padding to accommodate native device notches and navigation bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined, physical depth model rather than aggressive blur or opaque shadows. The feeling is that of matte-coated vellum cards resting cleanly over warm earthen plains.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base - `#FAFAF7`)**: Global canvas level. Holds layout grids and structural page divides.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards - `#FFFFFF`)**: Itinerary cards, weather summaries, chat bubbles, and profile panels. Outlined with a crisp, low-contrast border (`1px solid #E2E6DE`) and softened with an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive & Floating Panels)**: Bottom sheets, flyout route detail cards, and contextual action trays. Shadow profile: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Overlay Shells & Modals)**: Critical incident notifications, trilingual language sheets, and image lightboxes. Shadow profile: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(28, 30, 27, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a 30% tinted backdrop mask (`rgba(28, 30, 27, 0.4)` with 4px backdrop blur).

### Border Craft
No surface relies solely on a shadow for separation. All cards incorporate a 1px perimeter outline tinted slightly with forest undertones (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`) to maintain visual legibility in outdoor bright-light environments.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2**, striking a balance between consumer friendliness and infrastructural precision.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radii (4px - 6px)**: Checkbox squares, status notification micro-dots, progress bar fills, and compact table cells.
- **Component Radii (`rounded`, 8px - 10px)**: Input fields, language toggle buttons, dropdown menus, and nested media previews.
- **Card & Surface Radii (`rounded-lg`, 16px)**: Route summary containers, weather advisory widgets, map card previews, and AI conversational message blocks.
- **Structural Radii (`rounded-xl`, 24px)**: Top-anchored sheet modals, mobile bottom sheets, and promotional adventure banners.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Interactive action buttons, incident verification tags, transit chip filters, and floating audio-guide play controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Forest Green (`#1B4332`) fill, white text, pill-shaped (`9999px`), 48px height for touch targets. Hover state shifts to `#133E2C` with a slight `0 4px 12px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.18)` lift.
- **Secondary / Accent**: Warm Amber (`#D97706`) fill with pure white label. Used for real-time translation triggers, primary travel tips, and booking confirmations.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill, 1px border (`#E2E6DE`), charcoal text. Subtle `#F3F4F0` fill on hover.

### Multilingual Selector
- Segmented pill container (`#F3F4F0`) housing three inline touch tabs: `EN` (English), `አማ` (Amharic), and `OM` (Afaan Oromo).
- The active language transitions via an animated white surface badge (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px hairline border and subtle resting shadow. Typography auto-adjusts its font metric line-height upon toggle.

### Chips & Status Pills
- **Incident Status Pills**:
  - *Verified*: Soft green surface (`#ECFDF5`), deep emerald text (`#065F46`), leading check-circle icon.
  - *Under Review*: Soft amber surface (`#FEF3C7`), burnt amber text (`#92400E`), leading pulsating timer icon.
  - *Hazard / Alert*: Soft rose surface (`#FEE2E2`), crimson text (`#991B1B`), leading hazard triangle.
- **Filter Chips**: 32px height, 16px radius, neutral gray surface with dark charcoal text. Active state adopts `#1B4332` with white label.

### Travel Cards & Route Summaries
- **Card Enclosure**: Pure white surface, 16px radius, 1px perimeter line (`#E2E6DE`). Internal padding set to `space-lg` (24px).
- **Route Node Indicator**: A vertical continuous 2px dashed guide-line (`#CBD5E1`) linking step dots. Origin node uses an open ring (`#1B4332`); transfer points use solid mini dots (`#94A3B8`); terminal destination node is an amber map marker pin.

### Conversational AI Chat Bubbles
- **User Message**: Aligned right, Highland Green fill (`#1B4332`), white text, 18px radius with a bottom-right corner taper (4px).
- **Assistant Message**: Aligned left, `#F3F4F0` warm slate fill, `#1C1E1B` text, 18px radius with a bottom-left taper. Features an inline secondary source drawer indicating local validation (e.g., "Verified with local transport guild in Bahir Dar").

### Inputs & Form Fields
- 48px standard touch height, 10px corner radius, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E6DE`.
- Focus state highlights with a 2px ring in Forest Green (`#1B4332`) with zero offset. Field labels incorporate dual script subtitles when configured in dual-display accessibility mode.

### Weather & Altitude Widgets
- Translucent container with internal split: Left side displays current atmospheric metric (temperature, icon, altitude meter), right side displays dynamic apparel guidance pill (e.g., *"Gabi / warm shawl recommended for highland evening chill"*).
